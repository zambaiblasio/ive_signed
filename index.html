Right now, across Uganda, millions wake up to the same heavy question.

Will I make rent this month?

It is a question that quietly steals sleep, swallows savings, and forces families to choose between shelter and supper. Rent has become the most demanding burden on the ordinary citizen — and it is not letting go.

Hon. Zambali Blasio is proposing a bill in Parliament to freeze rent for 2 million Ugandans — in batches — for a full year (12 months), fully paid by the Government to landlords.

The bill is yet to reach Parliament. Based on current indications, it is expected to pass.

We are at the stage of collecting signatures from real persons who second the bill — to prove it is true and helpful towards citizens.

This is where you come in. Every verified name, every genuine signature, moves the bill closer to the floor of Parliament. It is not just a form — it is your voice, counted.

So what does this actually mean for you?

At least a year's rent holiday. No rent stress. A chance to breathe, to recover, and to mend your finances.

Hon. Zambai intends to support struggling members of society by lifting the most demanding burden off their shoulders — rent. For twelve full months, the Government of Uganda pays your landlord. You keep your home, and you keep your peace.

The focus is simple: Government lifts the rent burden so citizens can rebuild.

This programme is open to every Ugandan citizen who needs relief from rent — whether you are employed, self-employed, or currently without work.

The opportunity is here. Twelve months of rent, covered by Government.
